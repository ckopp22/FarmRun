Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Farm Run 3D ("the game") is made by Kopp Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the game handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to any server to send your data.

Data on your device

Your progress, farm, and settings are saved only on your own device. This data never leaves your device, and deleting the app or clearing its data erases it.

Third parties

The game does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children

Because we collect no personal information, the game does not knowingly collect data from children of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
